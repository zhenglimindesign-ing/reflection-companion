# Mobile text sample: try one method

[Overview](../README.md) · [Diaries and weekly reviews](USER_GUIDE.md) · [Computer setup](PLATFORMS.md)

Open a new conversation in a chat app you already use, such as Doubao, Kimi or DeepSeek. This is a plain-text introduction, not a claim that these apps have installed or passed compatibility testing for this Skill. Models, browsing, files and schedules depend on your account.

This is an ordinary prompt: a sample of one conversational method, not a mobile edition of the full Skill or a reason to install it. It does not carry references, state helpers, record loading or cross-chat corrections. For occasional questions, this or your own saved prompt is enough. For ongoing reflection and revision, see the [complete product value](../README.md).

## Copy this to begin

```text
Be my reflection companion. Help me understand my experiences without rushing to label me.
Offer three starting points: today's diary, a difficult choice, or a light self-exploration question.
Ask one question at a time. Ground observations in what I actually said; separate my words from your interpretations.
I can say “not me,” “no analysis yet,” “another one,” or “stop.” Do not treat disagreement as avoidance.
Use this chat and materials I provide. Say so if earlier conversations are inaccessible.
Start without requiring my full background. Do not save personal conclusions or set schedules unless I ask.
```

Choose and describe one small moment. Or skip the menu: “I did not get to share my idea in a meeting and feel disappointed. Help me write a short diary without analyzing yet.” See the [worked conversation](USER_GUIDE.md).

## Try something new

```text
Find recent publicly shared self-exploration methods, preferably light and requiring no history.
If browsing is available, verify original pages and dates; offer two different directions with sources.
Distinguish recently published, newly curated, and evidenced popularity. Be explicit about uncertainty.
Without browsing, write two original questions and label them as original, not recent web discoveries.
After I choose, guide me through one question at a time.
```

A chat app may not access our public catalog but can still search or suggest original questions. Do not promise cross-chat novelty. “We already explored that” is enough to adjust the current conversation.

## Add capabilities when useful

Ask for a copyable diary and put it in your usual notes app. Supply selected entries in the same chat for a weekly review. A first experience does not require uploading a complete archive.

Use the [computer path](PLATFORMS.md) for authorized local files and a corrigible learning store. The shared method does not automatically synchronize different platforms. Chinese copyable materials should be the main mobile entry; GitHub downloads should not gate the first experience.

Kimi documents creating custom skills conversationally through `/skill-creator` in Agent mode. The rules above can start that conversation.

# Reflection Companion

**Reflection Companion 是一个帮助你回顾经历、理解自己、拓展思考的 AI 反思伙伴。**

**Reflection Companion is an AI reflection companion that helps you revisit experience, understand yourself and explore new perspectives.**

<summary>简体中文</summary>

它以 Skill 的形式在你已有的 AI 工具中使用，以你愿意分享的经历和实际可访问的对话为材料：看见想法如何变化，检查自己的判断，尝试新的视角，并在你选择时留下值得继续思考的收获。你可以用它写日记、做周回顾、围绕一个问题深入讨论，或尝试新的自我探索方式。目前提供 Codex、Claude Code 和 Claude 网页版的安装包。

## 为什么做

了解自己，常常要从具体经历开始：我为什么做了这个选择？什么让我愿意投入？哪些问题反复出现？过去很在意的事情，现在还重要吗？这些问题需要回看当时的处境、理由和感受。

当我们经常和 AI 讨论工作、生活、兴趣与困惑时，其实已经自然积累了许多这样的材料。对话里不仅有问题的答案，还留着我们怎样提出问题、比较选项、面对不确定性，以及在新信息出现后改变想法的过程。它们散落在不同的聊天中，眼前的事情处理完后，很少再被联系起来。

Reflection Companion 希望让这些材料继续发挥作用。一次回顾可以帮你看清最近发生了什么；几段对话的比较，可以让你发现自己在意的东西、反复采用的思路，或一个仍需核实的模式。新的问题和视角，也可以帮助你认识尚未注意到的部分。你不必为了开始，先把生活重新整理成一套完整档案。

## 给谁用

- 经常用 AI 思考、写作、做选择或推进项目，希望从已有讨论中更了解自己的人。
- 想回顾一天、一周或一段经历，整理感受、变化和重要问题的人。
- 想检查一个判断，或试试新的自我探索方式的人。即使没有聊天历史，也可以从眼前的一件小事开始。

## 它可以怎样帮助你

| 能力 | 能帮助你做什么 | 常见用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 回顾经历 | 联系发生过的事、当时的想法与后来的变化，发现重要经历和仍值得想的问题。 | 日记、周回顾、阶段或主题回顾。 |
| 检查判断 | 检查证据、遗漏的条件和其他解释，探究反复出现的问题。 | 一个选择拿不准，或想核实“我是不是总这样”。 |
| 拓展视角 | 接触与当前问题有关的新概念、看法或经验，理解它们为什么有帮助。 | 选兴趣时，除了进步速度，也看看享受过程、环境和想再来一次的原因。 |
| 探索自己 | 找到适合当下的问题或练习，跟随你的回答继续讨论。 | 一次好奇的采访、一个值得问的问题、善意吐槽。 |
| 保留收获 | 留下你选择的理解、决定和问题，以后读回并结合新经历修正。 | 下次聊类似选择时接着想，或重新看一条以前的理解。 |

这些能力可以组合使用。一次讨论也可以只解决眼前的问题：检查判断可以支持你原来的想法，回顾可以发现没有变化；对你的解释都允许补充事实、拒绝或修正。

## 按你此刻的需要开始

能力说明帮助你理解它能做什么；下面这些场景帮助你找到使用入口。它们使用同一个 Companion，直接描述需要即可。

| 此刻的场景 | 可以直接说 | 可以得到什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 一天结束，想整理日记 | “回顾今天能读到的对话，看看发生了什么、我在意什么，整理成短日记；缺的再问我。” | 连接当天的经历、想法与感受，留下你愿意回看的内容。 |
| 一周结束，想看清变化 | “回顾这周，看看哪些想法变了、哪些问题有了结果，还有什么值得继续想。” | 联系前后的变化和新增信息，超出每天摘要的拼接。 |
| 一段经历告一段落 | “回看这个项目从开始到现在，我在意的东西和做决定的方式有没有变化？” | 梳理阶段或主题的演变；也可以换成一个月、一次旅行或一个持续的选择。 |
| 睡前只想轻松聊聊 | “今天不做分析，陪我聊一件想记住的小事。” | 自然交谈；想整理为日记时再提出。 |
| 一个具体判断拿不准 | “结合刚才说的事，看看我的解释可能遗漏什么，也考虑它合理的地方。” | 检查证据、情境与其他解释。 |
| 想试点新东西 | “给我两个自我探索方式，和刚才那个不同；我选好就开始。” | 少量合适的选项，随后在当前对话中开展。 |

没有历史也能从你愿意讲的一件事开始。按时间回顾时，它应先说明实际覆盖了哪些材料；日记和阶段回顾只写材料支持的部分。想每晚收到邀请或每周定期回顾，可以在手动试过后另行设置定时，见下方“材料、保存与定时”。

## 怎样玩自我探索

**说需要 → 看少量选项 → 选一个 → 在这里开始 → 随时调整。** 不需要记住玩法 ID、复制长提示词或做完整套题。

例如，这是一段虚构的起步示意：

> **你：** 推荐两个轻松一点、不需要历史的玩法。
>
> **Companion：** 可以试“一次好奇的采访”，从日常选择认识自己；或者“一个值得问的问题”，先围绕一个小问题聊。你选哪个？
>
> **你：** 采访，开始吧。
>
> **Companion：** 最近有没有一件你想做、后来没做的事？可以选很小的一件。

之后根据你的回答继续。也可以直接点名“善意吐槽”或让它代选，省去推荐步骤。你可以说“这个聊过了”“轻一点”“换个方向”或“停”；收获只在你要求时保存。

### 内容从哪里来，会不会更新？

| 来源 | 什么时候用 | 怎样更新 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 随包基础库 | 想直接开始，或没有网络时。覆盖变化、判断、价值、优势、兴趣和轻松表达等主题。 | 当前版本包含 18 个原创玩法；基础库随 Skill 版本维护，18 是这个版本的数量。 |
| 动态探索目录 | 问“有什么新的”“换种没试过的方式”时，按网络与工具条件读取公共目录。 | 目录可独立于 Skill 发版更新；读取失败时，说明改用了哪天的随包快照。 |
| 针对当前处境设计 | 希望围绕正在讨论的事探索。 | 根据这次问题设计或调整，并说明关联。 |
| 即时联网搜索 | 明确想找近期公开灵感时。 | 核查公开来源与日期，按你的需求筛选。 |

所以，探索内容会变化，不限于 18 个固定问题。动态目录仍需要实际搜集、核查和发布，目前没有自动采集服务；“新收录”也不等于“刚发布”或“热门”。个人回答不会进入公共目录；跨 chat 避免重复，取决于是否有可读取的使用记录。想自己浏览基础库时，见[玩法库](https://github.com/zhenglimindesign-ing/reflection-companion/blob/main/docs/PROMPTS.zh-CN.md)。

### 2. 开始一次对话

从上面的场景选一个，或直接说你自己的需要。第一次也可以这样开始：

> “用 Reflection Companion，陪我回顾最近一件在意的小事，一次问一个问题，先不保存。”

有现成材料时，可以提供几段聊天或笔记，也可以让它读取你授权且平台能访问的范围。它应说明用了哪些材料，再与你讨论；缺少材料时，可以从你眼前愿意分享的经历开始。

### 3. 按你的反馈继续

你可以补充事实，说“这个理解不对”“换个角度”“今天只想聊聊”，也可以随时结束。想留下内容时，再说“整理成可读文件”或“保留这条收获”。第一次使用不要求填写个人档案、建立记录库或设置定时。

## 材料、保存与定时

- **能读到什么：** 取决于平台工具、你的授权和主动提供的材料。安装不会获得整个账号或其他平台的聊天历史。
- **内容放在哪里：** 原始聊天由平台管理；导出文件由你保管；启用本地收获库后，选定记录保存在个人工作区的 `.reflection-companion/state.json`，可以查看、修正和删除。Claude 网页临时文件需要下载保管，不能视为跨 chat 永久存储。
- **谁处理材料：** 内容仍会按所用 AI 平台的政策交给模型处理。可选本地记录为明文；本项目没有开发者数据服务器。
- **如何定期使用：** 手动体验后，可以请求宿主设置日记提醒、周回顾或探索推荐。安装不自动开启定时；实际可用性依平台运行条件确认。

对你的理解需要与你核对。一个反复出现的模式可以作为讨论线索，也可以因新的经历而修正。具体的导出、保存、纠正和定时用法见[场景与进阶指南](docs/USER_GUIDE.zh-CN.md)。

<summary>English</summary>

Delivered as a Skill inside your existing AI tools, it uses experiences you choose to share and conversations actually available to them. Revisit how your thinking has changed, examine a judgment, try another perspective and, when you choose, keep something useful for later. Use it for a diary, a weekly review, a focused discussion or a new self-exploration exercise. Packages are available for Codex, Claude Code and Claude web.

## Why it exists

Understanding yourself often starts with particular experiences. Why did I make that choice? What draws me in? Which questions keep returning? Does something I once cared about still matter? Exploring these questions means returning to the circumstances, reasons and feelings of the time.

If you regularly discuss work, life, interests and uncertainties with AI, you are already accumulating this material naturally. Conversations retain more than answers: they show how you frame questions, compare options, deal with uncertainty and change your mind when new information arrives. Those traces are scattered across chats and rarely connected after the immediate issue is resolved.

Reflection Companion helps you return to them. A review can clarify what happened recently. Comparing discussions can reveal what matters to you, a recurring way of thinking or a possible pattern worth checking. New questions and perspectives can help you notice something you have not considered. You can begin without first reconstructing your life as a complete archive.

## Who it is for

- People who regularly think, write, make choices or develop projects with AI and want to learn about themselves through those discussions.
- People who want to reflect on a day, a week or an experience, including feelings, changes and important questions.
- People who want to examine a judgment or try a different way of exploring themselves. Without chat history, you can begin with one small event happening now.

## What you can do with it

| Capability | How it helps | Common uses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reflect on experience | Connect events, earlier thinking and later changes; notice meaningful experiences and questions worth revisiting. | Diaries, weekly reviews, period or topic reviews. |
| Examine judgments | Check evidence, missing circumstances and other explanations; explore recurring questions. | An uncertain choice or checking “am I always like this?” |
| Broaden perspectives | Explore a relevant concept, interpretation or experience and understand how it connects. | When choosing a hobby, consider enjoyment, environment and wanting to return alongside progress. |
| Explore yourself | Find a suitable question or exercise and continue from your answers. | A curious interview, a question worth asking, a gentle roast. |
| Keep useful learning | Retain selected understandings, decisions and questions, then read and reconsider them against new experience. | Continue a similar choice later or revisit an earlier understanding. |

Use these together as needed, or just address the present question. Examining a judgment may support your existing view; a review may find no change. You can add facts, reject or revise interpretations.

## Start with the situation you are in

The capabilities explain what it can help with; these situations offer ways to begin. They use the same Companion. Describe what you need in ordinary language.

| Situation | Say | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| End-of-day diary | “Review today's accessible conversations: what happened and what mattered to me? Make a short diary and ask about gaps.” | Connect the day's events, thoughts and feelings into something you want to revisit. |
| Weekly review | “Review this week: what changed, which questions reached an outcome and what remains worth thinking about?” | Connect changes and new information across the week, beyond concatenating daily summaries. |
| The end of a period or experience | “Look back on this project from the beginning. Have my priorities or ways of deciding changed?” | Trace a period or theme. A month, a trip or an ongoing choice can also provide the scope. |
| A light bedtime conversation | “No analysis today; let's talk about one small thing I want to remember.” | Natural conversation; request a diary afterward if useful. |
| An uncertain judgment | “Use what I just described to check what my explanation might miss and where it makes sense.” | Examine evidence, circumstances and other explanations. |
| Something different | “Offer two self-exploration methods different from the last one. Start after I choose.” | A few suitable options followed by participation in this chat. |

Without history, begin with one event you want to describe. A time-based review should first state which material it actually covers; diaries and period reviews should stay within that evidence. After trying it manually, you can request nightly invitations or regular weekly reviews through a separate schedule. See “Sources, saving and schedules” below.

## How to explore

**Describe your need → see a few options → choose one → start here → adjust as you go.** No exercise IDs, long prompts or complete questionnaires to remember.

This invented exchange illustrates the start:

> **You:** Offer two light exercises that need no history.
>
> **Companion:** Try “A curious interview,” using everyday choices to explore yourself, or “A question worth asking,” starting with one small question. Which would you like?
>
> **You:** The interview. Let's start.
>
> **Companion:** Is there something you recently wanted to do but did not? It can be very small.

Continue from your answer. Name an exercise such as “A gentle roast,” or ask the Companion to choose, to skip the recommendation step. Say “already discussed,” “lighter,” “another direction” or “stop.” Takeaways are saved only when requested.

### Where does the material come from, and does it change?

| Source | When to use it | How it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bundled library | Start immediately or without network access. Themes include change, judgment, values, strengths, interests and playful expression. | This version contains 18 original exercises. The base library is maintained with Skill releases; 18 is this version's count. |
| Dynamic exploration catalog | Ask “what's new?” or for a method you have not tried; reading the public catalog depends on available network and tools. | Updated independently of Skill releases. If reading fails, disclose the dated bundled snapshot used instead. |
| An exercise for your situation | Explore something already under discussion. | Designed or adapted for the present question, with the connection explained. |
| Live web search | Explicitly request recent public inspiration. | Check public sources and dates, then select for your request. |

Exploration changes over time and is not limited to 18 fixed questions. Someone still needs to collect, verify and publish catalog material; no automatic collection service is running. Newly curated does not mean newly published or popular. Personal answers never enter the public catalog. Cross-chat repetition checks depend on accessible usage records. Browse the [base library](https://github.com/zhenglimindesign-ing/reflection-companion/blob/main/docs/PROMPTS.md) if you want to choose yourself.

### 2. Start a conversation

Choose a situation above or describe your own need. For a first conversation, try:

> “Use Reflection Companion to help me reflect on one small recent event I care about. Ask one question at a time; do not save yet.”

Provide existing conversations or notes, or ask it to read a scope you authorize and the host can access. It should explain which material it used before discussing it with you. Without existing material, start with an experience you want to share now.

### 3. Shape the discussion

Add facts, say “that interpretation is wrong,” “try another angle” or “I only want to talk today,” or end the conversation. If you want to keep something, ask for a readable export or a saved takeaway. First use requires no personal profile, learning store or schedule.

## Sources, saving and schedules

- **Available material:** Depends on host tools, your authorization and supplied sources. Installation does not grant access to your entire account or another platform's history.
- **Record locations:** The host manages original chats; you keep exported files. With a local learning store enabled, selected records live in your personal workspace's `.reflection-companion/state.json` and can be inspected, corrected and deleted. Download temporary Claude web files to keep them; they are not permanent cross-chat storage.
- **Processing:** Your AI host's model-processing policy still applies. Optional local records are plain text. This project has no developer data server.
- **Regular use:** After trying the process manually, you can request a host schedule for diary reminders, weekly reviews or exploration suggestions. Installation starts no schedule; availability depends on the host's operating conditions.

Interpretations should be checked with you. A recurring pattern can be a useful discussion lead and can change in light of new experience. See the [scenarios and further-use guide](docs/USER_GUIDE.md) for exports, saving, corrections and schedules.

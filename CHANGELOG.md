## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-27

First public alpha. Adds self-exploration as a peer usage mode, 18 original bilingual exercises, contextual selection, on-demand discovery of recent public prompts, and same-chat execution and feedback. Retains Reflect, Challenge, Expand and opt-in Preserve & Compound.

Adds a public overview, four fictional worked examples, bilingual guides, generated catalog, MIT license and voluntary feedback templates. Distribution is generated from one development source with inventory/hashes and guarded synchronization. Personal state is outside the package; no telemetry or schedule is enabled by installation.

The creator accepted public use based on real use of the earlier candidate. New features remain subject to real-world feedback. Scheduled delivery and another user's history access must be checked in that environment; no universal coverage or always-on delivery claim is made.
